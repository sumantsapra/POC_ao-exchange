Headline speakers

Jake Humphrey

Broadcaster, Author & Entrepreneur

Jake Humphrey is one of the UK’s most trusted and respected voices on performance, leadership, resilience and modern mindset. A broadcaster, author, entrepreneur and host of the popular podcast, ‘High Performance’, Jake has spent more than twenty years working at the highest levels of elite sport and business, shaping conversations around what it truly means to perform at your best. Jake’s career in media and elite sport started at CBBC, he then went on to become the youngest ever host of Match of the Day, before fronting the BBC’s award-winning Formula 1 coverage, Sports Personality Of The Year and hosting major Olympic Games programming. He led BT Sport’s Premier League and Champions League broadcasts for ten years. Jake lives in Norwich with his wife Harriet and their two children.

Gary Vaynerchuk

(Virtual)

Founder

VaynerMedia

Gary Vaynerchuk is a serial entrepreneur, Chairman of VaynerX, and CEO of VaynerMedia. Widely regarded as one of the leading global minds on future cultural and internet trends, Gary—known as “GaryVee”—is celebrated for his ability to spot early patterns that shape markets and consumer behavior. An accomplished angel investor, his early-stage portfolio includes Facebook, Twitter, Tumblr, Venmo, Snapchat, Coinbase, and Uber. Through VaynerMedia, his full-service global advertising agency, Gary helps Fortune 1000 brands build relevance across digital platforms, culture, and commerce. VaynerMedia operates within the VaynerX holding company, which includes VaynerProductions, Gallery Media Group, The Sasha Group, Tracer, VaynerSpeakers, VaynerTalent, and VaynerCommerce. Gary is also the co-founder of VaynerSports, Resy, and Empathy Wines, successfully leading Resy and Empathy to acquisitions by American Express and Constellation Brands.

Avery Akkineni

Chief Marketing Officer

VaynerX

Avery Akkineni is the Chief Marketing Officer of VaynerX, where she leads all things “Vayner” brand and drives growth across its portfolio of modern-communication companies founded by Gary Vaynerchuk. A trailblazer in digital and growth marketing, she has spent seven years launching new companies, expanding VaynerMedia internationally, and leading Fortune 500 partnerships including Google, Diageo, Anheuser-Busch, and PepsiCo. She built VaynerMedia APAC from the ground up, scaling the region to 150+ employees across Singapore, Bangkok, Sydney, and Tokyo. In 2021, she founded Vayner3, an innovation consultancy focused on emerging technologies like AI and Web3, earning recognition including Ad Age’s Web3 Trailblazer and Business Insider’s AI Thought Leader, and she serves on Salesforce’s AI Council, Meta’s Creative Council, and TikTok’s For You Collective, sits on the board of PeacePlayers, and hosts CoinDesk’s weekly GenC podcast.

Dean Norbiato is the Marketing Director at Kia Australia, having joined the Korean automotive company in January 2019.
